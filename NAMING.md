# App naming

## Considered so far

- Chalk
- Chalk it up (CIU, weak abbreviation)
- Winners triangle
- Cue the Score (CTS)

## Single words (no abbreviation needed)

- **Frameball**: the ball that clinches a frame. Short, about winning, clearly snooker. Favourite.
- **Baize**: the table cloth. Short and classy.
- **Clearance**: clearing the table; also hints at settling who's better.
- **Respot**: a re-spotted black decides a tied frame, so it suits a rivalry tracker.
- **Scoreline**: plain and descriptive.

## Phrases that shorten well

- **Cue Card (CC)**: pun on "score card", easy to say.
- **Down to the Black (DTTB)**: great phrase, weak abbreviation. "On the Black" (OTB) shortens better.
- **Frame Count (FC)**: describes exactly what the app does.
- **Top Table (TT)**: suits a head-to-head or league feel.
- **Pink & Black (P&B)**: looks nice as a logo.

## Fun but riskier

- **Snookered**: memorable, but suggests losing.
- **147**: good icon, hard to search for.
- **Pot Black**: existing trademark and BBC show; avoid.

## Shortlist

1. Frameball
2. Cue Card
3. Baize

Each works without an abbreviation. Cue Card (CC) is a punchier alternative to Cue the Score (CTS).
